# Awesome Passive Income [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> Income that continues after the work that created it is finished.

Covers products you can sell, audiences you can own, software you can charge for, assets you can rent out, and investments that pay you while you are not working.

Very little income is passive at the start. Most of what is listed here needs weeks or months of upfront work, and some of it needs ongoing upkeep. Each section opens with a short note on who it suits and what the work looks like, so you can pick a path before picking a tool.

## Start Here

Four reads that explain how small, durable income streams are actually built.

- [1,000 True Fans](https://kk.org/thetechnium/1000-true-fans/) - Kevin Kelly's essay on earning a living from a small audience that buys everything you make.
- [Do Things that Don't Scale](https://www.paulgraham.com/ds.html) - Essay on recruiting the first customers by hand.
- [How to Get Startup Ideas](https://www.paulgraham.com/startupideas.html) - Essay on finding ideas by noticing problems you already have.
- [MAKE: Bootstrapper's Handbook](https://readmake.com/) - Book by an indie maker on building, launching, and charging for small products without funding.

## Digital Products

Suits people who can package knowledge or a skill into a download. The product takes days to weeks to make. Sales depend on an audience or a marketplace that sends traffic, so plan for marketing as well as making.

- [Gumroad](https://gumroad.com/) - Checkout and delivery for digital goods, with a storefront included.
- [Lemon Squeezy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/) - Merchant of record for software and digital products, including sales tax.
- [Payhip](https://www.payhip.com/) - Store for downloads, courses, and memberships, with EU tax handling.
- [Stan](https://stan.store/) - Link-in-bio storefront for selling downloads, courses, and bookings from a social profile.
- [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/sell) - Marketplace where printables, planners, and other instant downloads are found through search.
- [Teachers Pay Teachers](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/) - Marketplace where educators sell lesson plans and classroom materials.
- [Leanpub](https://leanpub.com/) - Publishes ebooks in progress and pays authors as the book grows.
- [Kindle Direct Publishing](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US) - Amazon's portal for self-published ebooks and paperbacks.
- [Draft2Digital](https://draft2digital.com/) - Distributes a self-published book to many ebook and print stores from one upload.
- [IngramSpark](https://www.ingramspark.com/) - Prints self-published books on demand and distributes them to bookstores and libraries.
- [ACX](https://www.acx.com/) - Pays royalties on audiobooks sold through Audible.

## Templates and Assets

Suits designers and developers. Each template takes days to build, and buyers find it through the marketplace's own search, so there is less marketing than with a standalone product. Expect to update items when the platform they target changes.

- [ThemeForest](https://themeforest.net/) - Marketplace for website themes and templates.
- [CodeCanyon](https://codecanyon.net/) - Marketplace for scripts, plugins, and app templates.
- [Creative Market](https://creativemarket.com/) - Marketplace for fonts, graphics, templates, and themes from independent designers.
- [UI8](https://ui8.net/) - Marketplace for UI kits, icons, and design resources.
- [Notion Marketplace](https://www.notion.com/templates) - Sells Notion templates, with Notion acting as merchant of record for approved creators.
- [Framer Marketplace](https://www.framer.com/marketplace/templates/) - Sells website templates and components built in Framer.
- [Webflow Marketplace](https://webflow.com/templates) - Sells website templates to Webflow users.
- [Unity Asset Store](https://assetstore.unity.com/) - Marketplace for models, tools, and plugins used by Unity developers.
- [Fab](https://www.fab.com/) - Epic's marketplace for 3D models, materials, and Unreal Engine assets, paying creators 88% of each sale.

## Stock Media and Music

Suits photographers, videographers, and musicians who already produce a lot of work. Each file earns little, so income comes from volume built up over months. Rates per sale have been falling, so treat this as a supplement.

- [Adobe Stock Contributor](https://contributor.stock.adobe.com/) - Pays royalties when customers license submitted photos, video, and illustrations.
- [Shutterstock Contributor](https://submit.shutterstock.com/) - Pays contributors when customers license their stock media.
- [Alamy Contributor](https://www.alamy.com/contributor/) - Sells stock and editorial photos and video.
- [Pond5](https://www.pond5.com/sell-media) - Marketplace for stock footage, music, and sound effects.
- [AudioJungle](https://audiojungle.net/) - Marketplace for music and sound effects.
- [DistroKid](https://distrokid.com/) - Distributes music to streaming services for a yearly fee and passes on the royalties.
- [CD Baby](https://cdbaby.com/) - Distributes music to streaming services and collects publishing royalties.

## Print on Demand

Suits designers who want to sell physical products without holding stock. Designs are quick to upload, but margins are thin and marketplaces are crowded, so niche designs and steady uploading matter more than any single design.

- [Printful](https://www.printful.com/) - Prints and ships apparel and other merch on demand.
- [Printify](https://printify.com/) - Connects a store to a network of print-on-demand providers.
- [Redbubble](https://www.redbubble.com/) - Marketplace that prints and ships your designs on clothing, stickers, and decor.
- [Merch on Demand](https://merch.amazon.com/) - Amazon's print-on-demand program for shirts and other products listed on Amazon.
- [Zazzle](https://www.zazzle.com/sell) - Marketplace for customizable print-on-demand products that pays designers a royalty.

## Courses and Memberships

Suits people who can teach something others pay to learn. A course takes weeks to record and sells for years if the topic stays current. Memberships pay monthly but need regular new content, so they are only partly passive.

- [Udemy](https://www.udemy.com/teaching/) - Course marketplace that pays instructors a share of sales to its own students.
- [Skillshare](https://www.skillshare.com/en/teach) - Pays teachers from a royalty pool based on minutes watched.
- [Podia](https://www.podia.com/) - Hosts courses, downloads, webinars, and memberships on one site.
- [Teachable](https://www.teachable.com/) - Hosts online courses with quizzes, coaching, and payment plans.
- [Thinkific](https://www.thinkific.com/) - Hosts online courses and communities on your own branded site.
- [Kajabi](https://www.kajabi.com/) - Combines courses, email, and a community on one platform.
- [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/) - Charges fans a monthly membership for ongoing work.
- [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/) - Collects tips, memberships, and shop sales through your own Stripe or PayPal account.
- [Buy Me a Coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/) - Sells tips, extras, and memberships to an audience.
- [Memberful](https://memberful.com/) - Adds paid memberships to a site you already run.
- [Circle](https://circle.so/) - Runs a paid community with discussions, events, and courses.
- [Skool](https://www.skool.com/) - Hosts a community and classroom that charges a monthly fee.
- [Outseta](https://www.outseta.com/) - Combines memberships, billing, email, and a help desk for bootstrapped software.

## Newsletters and Publishing

Suits writers willing to publish on a schedule for a year or more before income is meaningful. An archive of evergreen posts keeps bringing in readers, and the list you build can be sold to through every other section.

- [Ghost](https://ghost.org/) - Open-source publishing platform with memberships and paid newsletters.
- [Buttondown](https://buttondown.com/) - Hosts a newsletter, including paid subscriptions, in a plain editor.
- [beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com/) - Hosts a newsletter with paid subscriptions, a website, and an ad network.
- [Kit](https://kit.com/) - Email platform for creators, formerly ConvertKit, with automations and digital sales.
- [Substack](https://substack.com/) - Hosts newsletters and pays writers from paid subscriptions.
- [WordPress](https://wordpress.org/) - Open-source system most independent content sites are built on.

## Affiliate Programs

Suits content sites, newsletters, and channels that already help readers choose what to buy. Commissions are small per sale, so this works once you have steady traffic from search or an audience. Programs change their rates without notice, so avoid depending on one.

- [Amazon Associates](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/) - Pays a commission on qualifying Amazon purchases you refer.
- [Impact](https://impact.com/) - Tracks affiliate and creator partnerships for large brands.
- [Awin](https://www.awin.com/) - Global affiliate network that now includes the former ShareASale marketplace.
- [CJ](https://www.cj.com/) - Large affiliate network connecting publishers with retail and travel brands.
- [Rakuten Advertising](https://rakutenadvertising.com/) - Affiliate network for large retail and travel brands.
- [ClickBank](https://www.clickbank.com/) - Marketplace for digital products that pays affiliates high commissions.
- [PartnerStack](https://partnerstack.com/) - Runs partner and affiliate programs for software companies.
- [Skimlinks](https://www.skimlinks.com/) - Turns ordinary product links on a site into affiliate links automatically.
- [Sovrn Commerce](https://www.sovrn.com/commerce/) - Converts product links into affiliate links across thousands of merchants.
- [Geniuslink](https://geniuslink.com/) - Sends each visitor to their local Amazon or app store so international clicks still earn.
- [Rewardful](https://www.rewardful.com/) - Tracks affiliates for businesses that already charge with Stripe.
- [FirstPromoter](https://firstpromoter.com/) - Runs affiliate and referral programs for subscription software.

## Advertising and Sponsorships

Suits sites and newsletters with steady traffic. Display ads pay by volume and most networks set a minimum. Sponsorships pay more per reader, but each deal takes some selling.

- [Google AdSense](https://adsense.google.com/start/) - Places display ads on sites that meet Google's eligibility rules.
- [Ezoic](https://www.ezoic.com/) - Manages ads for sites too small for the premium networks.
- [Mediavine](https://www.mediavine.com/) - Manages ads for publishers that already have substantial traffic.
- [Raptive](https://raptive.com/) - Manages and optimizes ads for independent publishers, formerly AdThrive.
- [EthicalAds](https://www.ethicalads.io/) - Serves privacy-focused ads on developer and technical sites.
- [Carbon Ads](https://www.carbonads.net/) - Sells small ads on developer, design, and creator sites.
- [BuySellAds](https://www.buysellads.com/) - Sells direct sponsorships on developer newsletters, podcasts, and sites.
- [Passionfroot](https://www.passionfroot.me/) - Storefront and workflow for selling sponsorships in newsletters, podcasts, and videos.

## Video and Podcasts

Suits people who can publish regularly for months before a channel takes off. Older videos and episodes keep earning from ads and search long after release, which is where the passive part comes from.

- [YouTube Partner Program](https://www.youtube.com/creators/earn/youtube-partner-program/) - Pays eligible channels from ads, memberships, and other YouTube features.
- [Spotify Partner Program](https://support.spotify.com/us/creators/article/spotify-partner-program/) - Pays eligible video podcasts from ads and Premium viewing in supported countries.
- [OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com/) - Free, open-source screen and camera recorder.
- [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/) - Edits short-form video, with templates and captions.
- [Descript](https://www.descript.com/) - Edits video and podcasts by editing the transcript.
- [Pictory](https://pictory.ai/) - Turns scripts, articles, and slides into videos.
- [Runway](https://runway.com/) - Generates and edits video in a hosted model studio.
- [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/) - Synthesizes and dubs voices for narration.
- [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) - Designs thumbnails, posts, and simple videos.
- [Buffer](https://buffer.com/) - Schedules posts across social accounts.
- [TubeBuddy](https://www.tubebuddy.com/) - Researches keywords and tags for YouTube channels.
- [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/) - Researches keywords and tracks analytics for YouTube channels.

## Apps, Extensions, and Games

Suits developers. Building takes weeks to months, and most income comes from a few hits, so shipping several small titles beats polishing one. Platform stores bring traffic, but expect to fix bugs and keep up with policy changes.

- [Unity](https://unity.com/) - Game engine widely used to ship mobile games.
- [Godot](https://godotengine.org/) - Open-source game engine with no revenue share.
- [itch.io](https://itch.io/) - Sells games, assets, and tools at a price you set, with a revenue share you choose.
- [CrazyGames Developer Portal](https://developer.crazygames.com/) - Publishes Unity and HTML5 web games and shares ad revenue.
- [Poki for Developers](https://developers.poki.com/) - Publishes web games to a large audience and shares the revenue.
- [Google AdMob](https://admob.google.com/home/) - Serves in-app ads on Android and iOS.
- [AppLovin](https://www.applovin.com/en) - Sells and mediates ads for mobile games.
- [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/) - Runs in-app subscriptions for iOS and Android.
- [Superwall](https://superwall.com/) - Tests paywall designs and prices in mobile apps without a new release.
- [ExtensionPay](https://extensionpay.com/) - Adds one-time and subscription payments to browser extensions.
- [Chrome Web Store](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore) - Documentation for publishing and updating Chrome extensions.
- [Shopify Partners](https://www.shopify.com/partners) - Lets developers sell apps and themes to Shopify merchants.
- [Atlassian Marketplace](https://developer.atlassian.com/platform/marketplace/) - Sells apps for Jira and Confluence to Atlassian customers.
- [App Store Small Business Program](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/) - Cuts Apple's commission for developers under the annual earnings threshold.
- [Google Play service fees](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622) - States the commission Google Play charges on app sales.

## Software Billing and Licensing

For anyone charging for software. A merchant of record collects and pays sales tax and VAT for you, which matters as soon as you sell to more than one country.

- [Stripe](https://stripe.com/) - Payments and subscriptions for software sold directly, with optional tax calculation.
- [Paddle](https://www.paddle.com/) - Merchant of record for SaaS, covering tax, billing, and subscriptions.
- [Polar](https://polar.sh/) - Billing for software, including licenses, subscriptions, and one-time sales.
- [Freemius](https://freemius.com/) - Merchant of record and licensing for WordPress plugins, themes, and software.
- [Keygen](https://keygen.sh/) - Issues and validates software license keys, with a self-hosted option.
- [Lago](https://getlago.com/) - Open-source billing for usage-based and subscription pricing.

## Analytics and SEO

Search traffic is what keeps most content and product sites earning without new posts. These tools show what people search for and how your pages perform.

- [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console/about) - Shows which searches bring visitors to your site and flags indexing problems.
- [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/about) - Search performance and site audits for Bing.
- [Ahrefs Webmaster Tools](https://ahrefs.com/webmaster-tools) - Free site audit and backlink data for sites you own.
- [Google Trends](https://trends.google.com/trends/) - Compares how interest in search topics changes over time.
- [Exploding Topics](https://explodingtopics.com/) - Lists topics whose search interest is growing quickly.
- [Keywords Everywhere](https://keywordseverywhere.com/) - Shows search volume beside results in the browser.
- [Google Analytics](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics/) - Measures traffic on sites and apps.
- [Plausible](https://plausible.io/) - Measures site traffic without cookies or personal profiles.
- [Fathom](https://usefathom.com/) - Measures site traffic on a simple, privacy-friendly dashboard.
- [PostHog](https://posthog.com/) - Records product analytics, session replay, and feature flags.

## Renting Out Assets

- [Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/host/homes) - Rents out a spare room or property to short-term guests.
- [Turo](https://turo.com/us/en/list-your-car) - Rents out a car you are not using, with insurance options from the platform.
- [Neighbor](https://www.neighbor.com/) - Rents out spare storage or parking space in the United States.
- [JustPark](https://www.justpark.com/) - Rents out a driveway or parking space in the United Kingdom.

## Tax and Disclosure

- [Disclosures 101 for Social Media Influencers](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers) - U.S. Federal Trade Commission guide to disclosing paid endorsements and affiliate links.
- [Influencers' guide to making clear that ads are ads](https://www.asa.org.uk/resource/influencers-guide.html) - UK Advertising Standards Authority guide to labelling ads and affiliate links.
- [Self-Employed Individuals Tax Center](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/self-employed-individuals-tax-center) - IRS guidance on tax for freelance and side income in the United States.
- [Tax-free allowances on property and trading income](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/tax-free-allowances-on-property-and-trading-income) - UK rules for the yearly tax-free allowances on small trading and property income.
- [VAT One Stop Shop](https://vat-one-stop-shop.ec.europa.eu/index_en) - How sellers of digital services to EU consumers report VAT in a single return.

## Savings and Bonds

Lower returns than stocks, with little or no risk to the amount you put in. Useful for money you may need within a few years.

- [Treasury bills](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/marketable-securities/treasury-bills/) - Official guide to buying short-term U.S. Treasury bills.
- [Savings bonds](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/savings-bonds/) - Official guide to Series EE and Series I savings bonds.
- [Bankrate high-yield savings](https://www.bankrate.com/banking/savings/best-high-yield-interests-savings-accounts/) - Compares interest rates on U.S. savings accounts.
- [Premium Bonds](https://www.nsandi.com/products/premium-bonds) - UK government-backed savings that pay tax-free prizes instead of interest.
- [MoneySavingExpert savings](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/savings/savings-accounts-best-interest/) - Compares interest rates on UK savings accounts.
- [Raisin](https://www.raisin.com/) - Opens savings accounts at partner banks across Europe from one login.

## On-chain Yield

High risk. Smart-contract bugs, hacks, and price swings can wipe out the principal, and the rules and tax treatment vary by country. Only use money you can afford to lose.

- [Ethereum staking](https://ethereum.org/staking/) - Official explanation of how Ethereum staking works and how to start.
- [Lido](https://lido.fi/) - Liquid staking for Ethereum that returns a tradable token for staked funds.
- [Rocket Pool](https://rocketpool.net/) - Decentralized Ethereum staking that lowers the deposit needed to run a validator.
- [Aave](https://aave.com/) - Lends and borrows crypto assets on-chain.
- [Compound](https://www.compound.xyz/) - Pays interest on crypto assets supplied to the protocol.
- [Uniswap docs](https://developers.uniswap.org/docs) - Developer documentation for liquidity pools on the Uniswap exchange.
