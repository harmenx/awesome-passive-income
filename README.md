## Digital Products

- [Gumroad](https://gumroad.com/) - Checkout and delivery for digital goods, with a storefront included.
- [Lemon Squeezy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/) - Merchant of record for software and digital products, including sales tax.
- [Paddle](https://www.paddle.com/) - Merchant of record for SaaS, covering tax, billing, and subscriptions.
- [Polar](https://polar.sh/) - Billing for software, including licenses, subscriptions, and one-time sales.
- [Payhip](https://www.payhip.com/) - Store for downloads, courses, and memberships, with EU tax handling.
- [Leanpub](https://leanpub.com/) - Publishes ebooks in progress and pays authors as the book grows.
- [Kindle Direct Publishing](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US) - Amazon's portal for self-published ebooks and paperbacks.
- [ACX](https://www.acx.com/) - Pays royalties on audiobooks sold through Audible.
- [ThemeForest](https://themeforest.net/) - Marketplace for website themes and templates.
- [CodeCanyon](https://codecanyon.net/) - Marketplace for scripts, plugins, and app templates.
- [AudioJungle](https://audiojungle.net/) - Marketplace for music and sound effects.
- [Adobe Stock Contributor](https://contributor.stock.adobe.com/) - Pays royalties when customers license submitted photos, video, and illustrations.
- [Shutterstock Contributor](https://submit.shutterstock.com/) - Pays contributors when customers license their stock media.
- [Printful](https://www.printful.com/) - Prints and ships apparel and other merch on demand.
- [Printify](https://printify.com/) - Connects a store to a network of print-on-demand providers.
- [Teachers Pay Teachers](https://www.teacherspayteachers.com/) - Marketplace where educators sell lesson plans and classroom materials.

## Courses and Memberships

- [Podia](https://www.podia.com/) - Hosts courses, downloads, webinars, and memberships on one site.
- [Teachable](https://www.teachable.com/) - Hosts online courses with quizzes, coaching, and payment plans.
- [Kajabi](https://www.kajabi.com/) - Combines courses, email, and a community on one platform.
- [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/) - Charges fans a monthly membership for ongoing work.
- [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/) - Collects tips, memberships, and shop sales through your own Stripe or PayPal account.
- [Buy Me a Coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/) - Sells tips, extras, and memberships to an audience.
- [Memberful](https://memberful.com/) - Adds paid memberships to a site you already run.
- [Circle](https://circle.so/) - Runs a paid community with discussions, events, and courses.
- [Skool](https://www.skool.com/) - Hosts a community and classroom that charges a monthly fee.
- [Outseta](https://www.outseta.com/) - Combines memberships, billing, email, and a help desk for bootstrapped software.

## Newsletters and Publishing

- [Ghost](https://ghost.org/) - Open-source publishing platform with memberships and paid newsletters.
- [Buttondown](https://buttondown.com/) - Hosts a newsletter, including paid subscriptions, in a plain editor.
- [beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com/) - Hosts a newsletter with paid subscriptions, a website, and an ad network.
- [Kit](https://kit.com/) - Email platform for creators, formerly ConvertKit, with automations and digital sales.
- [Substack](https://substack.com/) - Hosts newsletters and pays writers from paid subscriptions.
- [WordPress](https://wordpress.org/) - Open-source system most independent content sites are built on.

## Affiliate Programs

- [Amazon Associates](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/) - Pays a commission on qualifying Amazon purchases you refer.
- [Impact](https://impact.com/) - Tracks affiliate and creator partnerships for large brands.
- [Awin](https://www.awin.com/) - Global affiliate network that now includes the former ShareASale marketplace.
- [Rewardful](https://www.rewardful.com/) - Tracks affiliates for businesses that already charge with Stripe.
- [PartnerStack](https://partnerstack.com/) - Runs partner and affiliate programs for software companies.

## Advertising

- [Google AdSense](https://adsense.google.com/start/) - Places display ads on sites that meet Google's eligibility rules.
- [Mediavine](https://www.mediavine.com/) - Manages ads for publishers that already have substantial traffic.
- [Raptive](https://raptive.com/) - Manages and optimizes ads for independent publishers, formerly AdThrive.
- [EthicalAds](https://www.ethicalads.io/) - Serves privacy-focused ads on developer and technical sites.
- [Carbon Ads](https://www.carbonads.net/) - Sells small ads on developer, design, and creator sites.

## Video and Podcasts

- [YouTube Partner Program](https://www.youtube.com/creators/earn/youtube-partner-program/) - Pays eligible channels from ads, memberships, and other YouTube features.
- [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/) - Edits short-form video, with templates and captions.
- [Pictory](https://pictory.ai/) - Turns scripts, articles, and slides into videos.
- [Descript](https://www.descript.com/) - Edits video and podcasts by editing the transcript.
- [Runway](https://runway.com/) - Generates and edits video in a hosted model studio.
- [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/) - Synthesizes and dubs voices for narration.
- [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) - Designs thumbnails, posts, and simple videos.
- [Buffer](https://buffer.com/) - Schedules posts across social accounts.
- [TubeBuddy](https://www.tubebuddy.com/) - Researches keywords and tags for YouTube channels.
- [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/) - Researches keywords and tracks analytics for YouTube channels.

## Mobile Apps and Games

- [Unity](https://unity.com/) - Game engine widely used to ship mobile games.
- [Godot](https://godotengine.org/) - Open-source game engine with no revenue share.
- [Google AdMob](https://admob.google.com/home/) - Serves in-app ads on Android and iOS.
- [AppLovin](https://www.applovin.com/en) - Sells and mediates ads for mobile games.
- [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/) - Runs in-app subscriptions for iOS and Android.
- [App Store Small Business Program](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/) - Cuts Apple's commission for developers under the annual earnings threshold.
- [Google Play service fees](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622) - States the commission Google Play charges on app sales.

- [Railway](https://railway.com/) - Hosts services, databases, and scheduled jobs.
- [Render](https://render.com/) - Hosts web services, workers, cron jobs, and databases.
- [Fly.io](https://fly.io/) - Runs apps on virtual machines close to users.
- [Cloudflare Registrar](https://www.cloudflare.com/domains/) - Registers domains at wholesale cost.
- [Namecheap](https://www.namecheap.com/) - Registers domains, with free privacy on many extensions.
- [n8n](https://n8n.io/) - Automates workflows and can be self-hosted.
- [Zapier](https://zapier.com/) - Connects web apps and runs workflows without writing code.
- [Make](https://www.make.com/) - Builds multi-step automations in a visual editor.
